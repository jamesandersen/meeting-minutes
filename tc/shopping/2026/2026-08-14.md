## 1. General Topics

- **Topic:** Introduction of new Tech Council Member
  - **Discussion:** Introduced Jordan Williams as the new Tech Council member for Amazon.
- **Topic:** Version 8 Release Scheduling and Coordination
  - **Discussion:** Discussed the launch timing of the version 8 release. Aligned by consensus to wrap up all pull requests by midweek (Wednesday) and target the following Monday (August 24, 2026) for the official version cut and release announcement, utilizing Thursday and Friday as a buffer.
- **Topic:** Domain Technical Councils Updates
  - **Notes:** Amit Handa reported that the Food DTC has announced its members and maintainers and will begin introductory sessions next week. For the Lodging DTC, nominations and selections have been finalized, and introductory proceedings will begin within the next two weeks. Payments DTC received an overwhelming response with over 25 nominations. The team is currently finalizing eligibility criteria and selection rubrics to determine council composition, with recommendations expected by next week.
- **Topic:** Measuring Ecosystem Success Metrics
  - **Notes:** Amit Handa introduced a proposal to establish tracking metrics covering developer experience, adoption, and community governance. Prasad Wangikar suggested including developer experience velocity and responsiveness metrics. Ilya Grigorik suggested incorporating more direct adoption signals, such as Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) flowing through the protocol and census data from the Chrome User Experience Report to monitor schema adoption, noting the need to distinguish between public and private deployments. Greg Smith emphasized the importance of diversity metrics within governance as a hallmark of successful open-source efforts, and Niranjan Manjunath recommended tracking protocol adoption metrics like task completion rates and transaction volume. The group agreed to separate metrics into layers (contributor growth vs. high-fidelity production metrics) and submit offline feedback to the proposal.
- **Topic:** Domain Working Group Update
  - **Notes:** James Andersen shared a status update on the Feed Domain Working Group (DWG). Amit Handa noted the next step is to collect a representative cohort of partners (such as platforms, businesses, and feed management specialists) using a secure online Form before returning to the Governance Council for formal approval. Ilya Grigorik advised evaluating candidates against the recently updated DTC selection criteria to ensure necessary expertise.
