## 1. General Topics

- **Topic:** Domain Working Group Governance and Nomination Procedures
  - **Discussion:** Reviewed the updated domain working group (DWG) nomination process. It was discussed that DWG charters must have a designated chair/co-chair from the DTC, a finite scope, and a concrete end date, with final approval by the Governing Council (GC).
- **Topic:** Catalog and Feed Domain Working Group Charter
  - **Discussion:** James Andersen and Greg Smith discussed establishing the Feed DWG to address bulk product discovery and product feed standards. It was agreed that James Andersen would create the draft template and Greg Smith would co-chair, with participation open to external industry experts.

## 2. Open Discussions & DTC PR Reviews

- **Topic:** Location Services Architecture
  - **Discussion:** Debated whether to advertise location services under a "common service" catchall versus advertising per capability. It was noted that existing service contracts allow for sub-service capability negotiation without requiring wholesale adoption of all services. Also discussed geographic data scope and decided that geodata is restricted to search filters in requests and excluded from response objects.
  - **Context:** [Locations service structure and geofencing](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/589)
- **Topic:** Fractional Quantities Strategy
  - **Notes:** Ilya Grigorik and Amit Handa discussed the fractional quantities PR, debating whether to optimize for integer units versus floating-point numbers. Ilya Grigorik advocated for correctness by design to prevent rounding issues, while Amit Handa emphasized the need for an optimization function that reduces ambiguity and improves readability.
  - **Notes:** Ilya Grigorik proposed decoupling step-size units from fulfillment units, asserting that while businesses may present and request orders in specific increments, the business itself remains the authority on fulfillment, allowing them to adjust if actual weights differ from requested increments.
  - **Context:** [feat!: define sale-basis steps for quantities](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/653)
- **Topic:** Payment Handler and Payment Terms
  - **Notes:** Reviewed payment handler PR 458 and payment terms, with Amit Handa seeking clarification on the implementation details regarding platform allow-lists for config URLs to ensure a good consumer experience, and Jing Li flagging recent feedback on payment terms regarding lodging use cases.
  - **Context:** [feat: Add Payment Authentication Actions](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/458)
- **Topic:** Generic Request Constraints
  - **Notes:** Ilya Grigorik introduced PR 655, a proposal for a generic mechanism for request constraints (such as freezing fields or quantities), aiming to solve payment-specific issues while creating a reusable primitive for other UCP areas. Daniel Wyckoff and Ilya Grigorik discussed shifting away from a "mixin" pattern for constraints toward a well-defined single entity that can be applied universally, noting that this might require some "magic" or special UCP field processing.
  - **Context:** [feat: add response-carried request constraints](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/655)
- **Topic:** Release Versioning and Restrictions
  - **Notes:** Amit Handa raised concerns about the current release versioning PR, which restricts capability advertising to a single version, arguing that it may be too restrictive for production deployments that require mixed versioning. The group discussed moving from a "must" requirement to a "should" recommendation. Jing Li, Ilya Grigorik, and Drew Olson discussed the risks of allowing mixed versions (e.g., supporting a newer cart version while using an older checkout version), agreeing that while the protocol should not strictly invalidate this, it is a difficult design space that requires clear communication to integrators regarding potential data-transfer challenges.
  - **Notes:** The group discussed a potential need to adjust how merchants onboard onto the search platform to accommodate the features being supported, ensuring that features are not mixed and matched inconsistently. A deep dive will be conducted to address onboarding guidelines.
  - **Context:** [docs: clarify UCP core release versioning contract](https://github.com/Universal-Commerce-Protocol/ucp/pull/614)
